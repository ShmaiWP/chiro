FAQ

Questions & answers

Something else on your mind? Email info@shmai.com

Who is this for?

A chiropractor who is also the front desk, or who has one person at the desk. One location. California offices are who we are writing to first.

What does the AI receptionist do?

It answers the call that comes in while you are with a patient. It books a name, a phone number and a time. It writes that lead into the system you already use.

Does it replace Jane, ChiroTouch or ChiroFusion?

No. If you already pay for one of those, the lead is written into it. We do not sell you a second scheduler.

Will it ask what hurts, or suggest care?

No. A box that collects symptoms is a privacy problem, and the receptionist does not give clinical advice.

What sits beside the receptionist?

A website with a tappable number and one way to ask for a visit. Upkeep so that path keeps working. SEO so a nearby patient can find the office. Social posts aimed at a new patient.

What does it cost?

Write to info@shmai.com. We do not put a price on this page.
